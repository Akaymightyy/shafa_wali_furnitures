Before you order

Frequently Asked

How do I place an order? +

Tap any "Inquire on WhatsApp" button or message +234 907 880 2181. Share the piece you want, your location, and preferred delivery date — our team confirms stock and final pricing within hours.

Do you offer custom sizes & finishes? +

Yes. Bespoke pieces are a core part of what we do — bring measurements, fabric swatches, or a reference photo and we'll quote a custom build. Custom pieces typically take 2–4 weeks depending on complexity.

What is your refund & exchange policy? +

Strictly No Refund, No Exchange. Please inspect items in our Kaduna or Zaria showrooms, or request a live WhatsApp video preview before completing payment. This protects both our artisans and our customers.

Do you deliver outside Kaduna State? +

Yes — we coordinate nationwide delivery through trusted logistics partners. Shipping cost and timeline are quoted per item and destination. Live tracking is shared once dispatch is confirmed.

Can I get a video preview before paying? +

Absolutely. Live WhatsApp video previews are encouraged — we'll walk you through the piece, dimensions, finish, and any defects (if applicable) before you commit.
